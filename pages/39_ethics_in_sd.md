# Ethical Considerations in Software Development

::content::

**Ethics** is the study of what is right and wrong, and how people should behave.

**Ethical considerations** are issues around deciding right and wrong - particularly when there is not always a clear answer.

There are lots of reasons why ethics is important in Software Development and the study design lists:

- Ineffective security practices
- Use of Artificial Intelligence during development
- Intellectual property
- Copyright issues
